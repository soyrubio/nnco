# NNCo. LinkedIn cover

Upload `nnco-company-cover.jpg` to the NNCo. company page. A PNG export and an
editable, self-contained SVG are also included. Both raster exports are
1512 × 256 pixels and below 3 MB, matching the [LinkedIn Page specifications](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a563309)
checked on 8 September 2026.

The final artwork uses the official Group 97 mark, Geist Medium, the site's
ink and paper colours, and the existing homepage headline. The left margin
leaves room for the page avatar. Check the crop in LinkedIn's upload preview.

The SVG embeds Geist for consistent rendering. Its license is in `OFL.txt`.
The exports were rendered from that SVG, so the logo and text are exact.
No generated logo or generated lettering is included in the final artwork.

An initial concept was explored with the built-in image generation tool using
this brief: create a restrained, monochrome NNCo. company cover with the
official mark, generous negative space and the exact headline “We design,
integrate and operate AI systems.” No people, gradients, 3D, circuitry or
additional copy. The native SVG is the final production source.
